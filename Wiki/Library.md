# Library

The Library is the primary chronological photo and video timeline.

## What you can do

- Browse photos and videos in a Metal-rendered grid.
- Change grid density and choose square or original-aspect thumbnails.
- Filter by Favorites, photos, or videos.
- Select multiple items for sharing, album actions, favorite changes, or deletion.
- Open any item in the [[Photo and Video Viewer|Photo-and-Video-Viewer]].
- Continue browsing while thumbnails and background indexes converge.

## Photos that are still backing up

While backup runs, photos that are not uploaded yet already appear in the Library, in their place by
capture date. A ring in the corner fills while the photo uploads, then a checkmark shows, and the tile
turns into the uploaded photo in the same place. On a Mac, files in watched backup folders appear the
same way.

These photos load from Apple Photos or the folder on this device; nothing is sent to Proton before the
upload. You can open, share, export, favorite, and add them to albums like any other photo. Favorites and
album additions apply after the upload.

Deleting such a photo takes it out of the backup on this device. The photo stays in Apple Photos or in its
folder. A notice offers **Undo**; on a Mac, **Edit → Undo** works too. The photo then waits in
[[Recently Deleted|Recently-Deleted]] and in the Backup settings under **Excluded from Backup**.

With backup turned off, the Library shows only uploaded photos.

## iPhone and iPad

Open the **Photos** tab. Use the account button for [[Settings, Cache, and Privacy|Settings-Cache-and-Privacy]], the options menu for filters and display settings, and **Select** for bulk actions.

## Mac

Choose **All Photos** or a filter in the sidebar. The toolbar owns display, selection, upload, and library controls. The Mac can also upload selected photos or a folder directly.

Offline or reconnecting states appear as library status, not as a second library. Cached media remains available according to the current cache settings.
