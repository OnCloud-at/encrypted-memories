# Collections

Collections groups shared library filters and album routes without creating separate copies of media.

## Built-in collections

- **iPhone and iPad:** Favorites, Videos, Live Photos, Recently Deleted, your albums, and shared-album listings.
- **Mac:** Favorites, Screenshots, Videos, Live Photos, Motion Photos, Selfies, Portraits, Bursts, Panoramas, RAW, Recently Deleted, your albums, and shared-album listings.

## iPhone and iPad

Open the **Collections** tab. Each row opens the same shared timeline filtered to that collection. Use the add menu to create an album when the account supports album creation.

## Mac

The wider smart-filter set, albums, shared-album listings, and Recently Deleted appear directly in the sidebar.

Continue with [[Albums|Albums]] or [[Recently Deleted|Recently-Deleted]].
