# Photo and Video Viewer

The Viewer opens photos, videos, Live Photos, and burst media without leaving the current library context.

## Common actions

- Move to the previous or next item.
- Zoom and pan images.
- Play and pause video.
- Press and hold a Live Photo to play motion where supported.
- Browse burst frames.
- Favorite or unfavorite an item.
- Share or export the original format.
- Move an item to Recently Deleted, or restore it when opened from trash.
- Open [[Media Information|Media-Information]].

Album changes are available from Library selection, not from the Viewer.

## Platform controls

- **iPhone and iPad:** Swipe between pages and use native touch, pinch, drag, and long-press gestures. Viewer actions use top and bottom chrome.
- **Mac:** Use mouse, trackpad, keyboard, and toolbar controls. The viewer preserves the selected sidebar context.

Decrypted originals are materialized only for an explicit share or export operation. Video playback reads encrypted cached ranges and decrypts requested data in memory.
