# Albums

Albums organize existing Proton Drive photos without duplicating their encrypted media.

Depending on backend capability, Encrypted Memories can:

- List and open your albums.
- Create albums.
- Add selected photos and videos.
- Remove an item from an album without deleting it from the library.
- Set an album cover.
- Delete an album after confirmation.
- List albums shared with you and leave a shared album when permitted.

Controls that the current account or backend cannot perform stay hidden or disabled. The Mac sidebar and the iPhone/iPad Collections navigation use the same album repository and mutation rules.

You can open an album shared with you and browse its photos. Shared albums are read-only: selection, trash, favorites and album actions are not offered there.

You can open an album shared with you and browse its photos. Shared albums are read-only: selection, trash, favorites and album actions are not offered there.

Each album shared with you shows your access: Viewer, Editor, Admin, or Inherited access. When Proton reports a direct invitation, the row also shows who invited you and when. An inviter whose invitation signature could not be verified is marked as unverified.

Albums shared with you are read-only in the app for every role:

- Viewers and inherited access cannot edit the album.
- Editors and admins cannot add or remove photos or change the cover here yet. The app's album editing only works with albums in your own Proton Drive. Use Proton Drive to edit an album shared with you.
- Sharing an album, inviting people, and changing roles are not available in the app.

Local Apple Photos album mirroring is separate. See [[Album Sync|Album-Sync]].
